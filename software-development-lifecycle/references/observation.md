# Production Outcome Observation

## Plan

- Define the outcome to measure.
- Define the baseline when available.
- Identify reliable success and failure signals.
- Identify required apps, data, logs, metrics, or feedback.
- Confirm privacy, security, retention, and access boundaries.
- Define who will observe the signals.
- Define the observation period or decision point.
- Define results that trigger keep, improve, rollback, or retirement discussion.
- Prefer the smallest reliable signal set.
- Do not invent numeric targets without evidence.

## Observe

- Begin only after an authorized release.
- Use only authorized production systems and data.
- Compare actual results with the Goal and baseline.
- Review agreed usage, feedback, errors, reliability, and performance signals.
- Record findings through a new documentation change.
- Update the existing plan.
- Keep the completed release document unchanged.
- Discuss whether to keep, improve, roll back, or retire the change.
- Add a new Step before implementing follow-up work.
